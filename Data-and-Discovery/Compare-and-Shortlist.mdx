Once a student has narrowed a list of institutions through search or browse, Compare and Shortlist are the tools that turn that list into a decision.

## Compare

A student can compare up to three institutions side by side. Comparison covers:

| Field group | What's compared |
| --- | --- |
| Rankings | NIRF rank and score across categories |
| Accreditation | NAAC grade and CGPA |
| Fees | Course-level tuition and total program cost |
| Placements | Average, median, and highest package |
| Courses | Programs offered at each institution |
| Faculty | Faculty count and featured profiles |
| Infrastructure | Hostel, library, sports, medical facility |
| Campus | Size, established year, student strength |
| Research | Where available |
| Scholarships | Eligibility and amount |

The higher or better value in each row is highlighted, so a student can scan for the winner on any given metric without doing the comparison manually. A finished comparison can be exported as a PDF or shared as a link.

## Shortlist

A student can save institutions to a shortlist while browsing, without logging in or providing any information. The shortlist persists for the session and is the natural bridge into Compare: institutions saved while exploring can be pulled directly into a side-by-side comparison later.

<Note>
  No login or personal information is required to search, compare, or shortlist. An account is only required to save a shortlist across devices and sessions, use PraveshAI, or apply for premium counseling.
</Note>

## Why comparison stays neutral

Compare inherits the same principle that governs ranking across all of Manifest: every field shown is either a government-published metric or a transparently enriched fact, and no institution can influence its position in a side-by-side comparison. A comparison on Manifest reflects the data, not a paid placement.
