Cost is one of the most searched, and least standardized, pieces of information a student looks for. Manifest tracks it at the course level, not just as a single figure per institution, since two programs at the same college can differ substantially in fee.

## Fees

Fee data lives in the course table, linked to the parent institution by AISHE code.

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Course name | The specific program |
| Level | UG, PG, or PhD |
| Annual tuition fee | Yearly cost for that course |
| Total seats | Seats available in that course |

The Courses & Fees section of a college profile presents the full course list by level, alongside total cost of the program, so a student can compare not just tuition but the full multi-year commitment before applying.

## Scholarships

The Scholarships section of a profile covers:

- Government scholarships available to students at that institution
- Institutional scholarships offered directly by the college
- Eligibility criteria and amount, where published

Scholarship data is enriched from the institution's own site, the same pipeline used for placements and infrastructure, and carries the same completeness indicator when a field has not yet been verified.

## How this supports decision-making

Fee and scholarship data on Manifest is built to answer a specific question a student actually asks: not "what is the tuition," but "what will this program cost me, and is there anything that reduces it." Presenting course-level fees next to scholarship eligibility, on the same profile, is what makes that question answerable in one place instead of across five different sources.
